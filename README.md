# Fidget Shooter Releases

Public distribution channel for protected stable Fidget Shooter builds.

## v41.53

[Download the playable HTML](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/minhhuy030309-rgb/fidget-shooter-releases/main/latest.html) and open the saved file in your browser. Choose Solo Duel, then **Gunslinger · Free Roam Prototype** and select Crossroads or Tutorial.

Crossroads now includes a tavern with civilians, campfire rest/manual saves/crafting, and a rideable horse with blended gaits, steering, pace and stamina. Tutorial is a mounted duel against an Outlaw, with a scrolling town, horse rolls and a victory ending. The destination lobby uses the earlier chase as its waiting scene.

This release also refines mobile controls/menus, bandage healing, aiming controls, elemental effects and embedded ambience/hoof audio. It incorporates the 102-suite campaign audit and final release/updater checks, including contact, layering, cleanup and mounted-rendering repairs. Physical-device performance and real-network multiplayer remain manual validation limits.

Multiplayer uses **protocol 88**; both players need compatible files. Campaign progress stays local to the browser/device. The Waystation supplies and ammunition grants remain prototype testing content. The original solo routes and multiplayer remain available.

The public HTML was downloaded and verified before announcing this release. Its SHA-256 is `299c12cce835a934459c68be54fcc7cb4b77c7e1512b7bb01fbe1fd6228169a5`.

Music attribution and license links are in campaign Settings → Credits. Development source and history live in the private `fidget-shooter` repository.
