# Fidget Shooter Releases

Public distribution channel for protected stable Fidget Shooter builds.

## v41.57

[Download the playable HTML](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/minhhuy030309-rgb/fidget-shooter-releases/main/latest.html?v=41.57) and open the saved file in your browser. For co-op, open multiplayer, choose co-op and select Gunslinger on both clients. Meet in the Cinder Club lounge and ready up to explore together. Co-op targets PC and landscape mobile. For single player, open **Gunslinger · Lobby**, then select **Tutorial**, **Crossroads** or **Flower Meadow**.

Gunslinger co-op exploration combat, personal loot and the Cinder Club lobby.

- Ready together in the Cinder Club lounge, then explore connected rooms as a Gunslinger party
- Fight Outlaws and knife-wielding Rogues directly in exploration, with armour, headshots, rolls, elemental ammunition and reactions
- The first player entering a room activates its enemies; trailing allies can still enter after combat seals the paths
- Collect flying coins and personal chest ammunition, and spend individual wallets at travelling merchants
- Discover varied cover layouts, breakable props, purchasable lassos, stealth executions and ambient sound
- Guest rendering recovers from transient room-mount errors; co-op targets PC and landscape mobile
- Campaign and PvP remain available; both multiplayer clients need protocol 136 builds

Multiplayer uses **protocol 136**; both players need compatible files.
